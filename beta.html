Wind & angle

Weather wind is measured 10 m (33 ft) above ground — usually stronger than the wind at shooter height (about ⅔ of it in open ground).
Drag around the dial's ring (or press and hold) to set where the wind comes from; tap an hour to snap to it. Use − / + for speed and direction, or tap a number to type it. 0° = wind in your face, 90° = from your right, 180° = from behind, 270° = from your left.
Leave 0 for a flat shot. Target distance is the rangefinder (line-of-sight) distance.

Load (ammo for this gun)
Load details
Many powders shift 1–2 fps per °F (10–20 per 10°F). MV used = chrono MV + sensitivity × (powder temp − chrono temp) ÷ 10; leave sensitivity blank or 0 to turn it off. Tip length (blank = estimated for tipped bullets) refines the stability check.
BC must match the drag model printed on the box (G1 vs G7). Bullet length is optional; it enables spin drift. Factory BCs are often optimistic — true them against real drops.
Estimate MV for a different barrel (no chronograph)
Estimate = reference MV + (your barrel − reference barrel) × fps per inch. Rough guide only (typically 20–30 fps/in); a chronographed MV always wins. Set your barrel length under Gun details.
